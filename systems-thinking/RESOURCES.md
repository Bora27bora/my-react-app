# Thinking in Systems Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Thinking in Systems: A Primer_ — Donella H. Meadows, ed. Diana Wright (Chelsea Green, 2008)](https://www.chelseagreen.com/product/thinking-in-systems/)
  The primer this whole workspace is built around. Use for: the canonical definitions of stocks, flows, feedback loops, system traps, and leverage points — all lessons should trace back to the chapter the user is currently reading.
- [Essay: "Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System" — Donella Meadows (1999)](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/)
  The original essay that Chapter 6 ("Leverage Points") expands into a full chapter. Shorter and freely readable — good as a primary-source citation once we reach leverage points.
- [The Donella Meadows Project Archive](https://donellameadows.org/archives/)
  Meadows's collected essays and "Dancing with Systems" — useful for connecting book concepts to her wider worldview (Part Three material especially).
- [Twelve Leverage Points — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_leverage_points)
  Quick-reference summary of the 12-point hierarchy from Chapter 6. Use for: double-checking the ordering/wording of leverage points against the secondary literature.
- [Systems Thinking — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_thinking)
  General field overview. Use for: cross-checking how terms from the book (feedback loop, stock, delay, etc.) are used in the wider systems-thinking field.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [System Dynamics Society](https://systemdynamics.org)
  The main international professional body for systems thinking / system dynamics. Publishes the *System Dynamics Review* and runs events. Use for: going beyond the book into formal modeling once the fundamentals are solid.
- [Academy for Systems Change (incl. The Donella Meadows Project)](https://www.academyforchange.org/)
  Successor to the Donella Meadows Institute. Runs the "Dancing with Systems" learning journey based on Meadows's later essays. Use for: applying systems thinking to real-world change efforts (Part Three territory).
- [Systems Community of Inquiry](https://stream.syscoi.com/) — runs periodic book clubs, including one on *Thinking in Systems*.
  Use for: discussing chapters with other readers once the user wants peer feedback on their understanding.
